# V0.4 — Mechanism Evidence Bridge

## Purpose and boundary

The Mechanism Evidence Bridge packages explicitly proposed mechanism hypotheses and validates their links to the canonical research bundle. It connects a user-selected predictive interaction to source evidence, direct observations, theoretical calculations, prospective observations, and contradictory records.

It does **not** discover a physical mechanism from feature names or predictive model coefficients. A user, Agent, or Worker must supply a mechanism plan. The engine validates IDs, Study relationships, source locators, extraction states, and evidence-role requirements; it then creates a traceable review package.

The initial implementation accepts numeric-endpoint pairwise interaction candidates from V0.4. A selected interaction must already have the status `CROSS_STUDY_PREDICTIVE_INTERACTION_CANDIDATE`. The mechanism bridge does not reinterpret negative or non-stable pairs as positive discoveries.

## Input contract: mechanism_plan.json

Create a plan from `templates/mechanism_plan.example.json` and validate it against `schemas/mechanism_plan.schema.json`.

The plan identifies the exact task, target, endpoint, and `interaction_id`. It contains one or more explicit hypotheses, each with:

- a unique `hypothesis_id`, title, and testable hypothesis statement;
- proposed process chain and scope statement;
- predicted observations, alternative explanations, and unresolved variables;
- explicit evidence assessments.

Each evidence assessment contains:
- exact canonical `evidence_id`;
- relationship: `SUPPORTS`, `CONTRADICTS`, or `CONTEXTUAL`;
- evidence class: `DIRECT_OBSERVATION`, `THEORETICAL_SUPPORT`, `PROSPECTIVE_VALIDATION`, `METHOD_CONTEXT`, or `OTHER`;
- concise interpretation summary;
- `observation_ids` when the class requires them.

`proposal_origin` records whether the plan is a `USER_DRAFT`, `AGENT_DRAFT`, or `WORKER_DRAFT`. This field is provenance, not approval. The plan is not treated as scientifically reviewed merely because it parses or passes validation.

## Evidence validation rules

All evidence IDs must exist in the supplied canonical bundle. The exact canonical bundle SHA-256 must match the bundle recorded when the modeling cohort was built. Evidence source file and locator are preserved in the ledger. Source locator type, file hash, file path, and locator text are structurally checked.

### Direct observation

A `DIRECT_OBSERVATION` assessment must include at least one canonical Observation ID. Each linked Observation must:
- exist in the same canonical bundle;
- reference the same Evidence ID in its `evidence_ids`;
- belong to the same Study as the Evidence record;
- have `value_state = REPORTED`; and
- contain a recorded raw or normalized value.

Direct evidence contributes to verified evidence summaries only when the canonical Evidence record has `extraction_status = VERIFIED`. Missing, `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, `CONFLICTING`, and `UNVERIFIED` states are retained but not counted as verified support.

### Theoretical support

A `THEORETICAL_SUPPORT` assessment must reference a canonical Evidence record with a valid source locator. The plan explicitly identifies it as theory, simulation, or mechanistic calculation support. The bridge does not infer theoretical relevance from filenames, quoted words, or evidence type metadata.

### Prospective validation

A `PROSPECTIVE_VALIDATION` assessment must link to REPORTED Observation records. Its Evidence Study ID must be outside the Study IDs used to evaluate the selected interaction, and any recorded DOI must not exactly match a DOI from those discovery Studies. DOI strings are compared exactly; the bridge does not silently normalize or repair DOI values.

If the Study ID or exact DOI overlaps the interaction-discovery cohort, the record remains in the evidence ledger and review queue but is not counted as independent prospective support. If the prospective Study has no DOI, the report records that independence was assessed by Study ID only, without a DOI cross-check.

A prospective record can either support or contradict the hypothesis. The class indicates the design/intended role, while the relationship records what the evidence says.

### Contradictory and contextual evidence

Contradictory evidence remains in the report and ledger with its original source locator, Study identity, and linked Observation IDs. It is never averaged with supporting evidence or silently removed. Contradictory evidence that is unverified, contextual, or unclassified remains in the review queue rather than being silently discarded.

Contextual/method evidence is retained as context and does not count as direct or theoretical mechanism support. Evidence classes are descriptive categories, not a universal ordinal hierarchy.

## Interaction, robustness, and response-surface provenance

The bridge verifies the interaction discovery report and manifest against the exact cohort and task. It also verifies the selected `interaction_id` and requires the predictive-candidate status.

Optional robustness and response-surface directories are supported:
- Repeated-seed robustness is linked as a split-sensitivity descriptor, not evidence of a mechanism.
- A response surface is linked as a descriptive visualization artifact only. It is not accepted as direct observation or theoretical evidence. Its all-cohort descriptive refit is not an independent validation result.

The optional packages must contain their expected report and manifest files, have matching report hashes, and match the exact cohort/task/interaction provenance.

## Evidence status vocabulary

Evidence summaries are descriptive combinations of explicit records, not a score or rank:

- `HYPOTHESIS_ONLY`: no evidence assessments supplied.
- `EVIDENCE_REVIEW_REQUIRED`: assessments exist but no verified classified support/contradiction can yet be counted.
- `DIRECT_OBSERVATION_SUPPORT_RECORDED`
- `THEORETICAL_SUPPORT_RECORDED`
- `DIRECT_AND_THEORETICAL_SUPPORT_RECORDED`
- `INDEPENDENT_PROSPECTIVE_SUPPORT_RECORDED`
- `CONTRADICTION_RECORDED`
- `SUPPORT_AND_CONTRADICTION_RECORDED`
- `NO_CLASSIFIED_MECHANISM_SUPPORT`

These states describe which evidence records are present and verified. They do not establish a causal mechanism or determine which explanation is correct.

## Command

After the user selects an interaction at Gate G3 and a mechanism plan has been prepared:

~~~bash
dor build-mechanism-evidence-bridge \
  --bundle 03_canonical/canonical_bundle.json \
  --cohort-dir 04_modeling/cohort \
  --task modeling_task.json \
  --interaction-dir 04_modeling/interactions \
  --plan mechanism_plan.json \
  --out-dir 05_mechanisms/bridge
~~~

Optional links:

~~~bash
  --robustness-dir 04_modeling/interaction_robustness \
  --response-surface-dir 04_modeling/interaction_surfaces
~~~

The output directory must be new or empty. All input files are hash-snapshotted before reading and re-hashed after construction.

## Output package

- `mechanism_evidence_bridge_report.json`: interaction identity, explicit hypothesis catalogue, evidence status counts, source evidence records, review queue, and claim boundaries.
- `mechanism_findings.json`: structured `research_finding.schema.json` records for downstream writing.
- `mechanism_evidence_ledger.csv`: one row per hypothesis/evidence assessment, including relationship, evidence class, Study/DOI, source file and locator, extraction state, independence basis, Observation links, and whether the record was counted.
- `mechanism_review_queue.csv`: unresolved source verification, non-independent prospective labels, contradictions, and hypotheses without classified support.
- `mechanism_evidence_bridge_manifest.json`: exact input and output hashes.

Schemas: `mechanism_plan.schema.json`, `mechanism_evidence_bridge_report.schema.json`, `mechanism_findings.schema.json`, and `mechanism_evidence_bridge_manifest.schema.json`.

## Mandatory scientific claim boundary

Every generated Finding remains:
- `claim_type = mechanism_hypothesis`;
- `status = candidate`;
- `mechanism_status = hypothesis`.

The report is always `PENDING_USER_MECHANISM_REVIEW`. Neither a predictive-candidate label, repeated-seed stability, a response surface, nor any evidence count automatically promotes a hypothesis to an established or validated mechanism.

Review the original source locators and counterevidence before writing mechanistic Results. Describe the pattern as a hypothesis unless independent evidence genuinely warrants stronger language. Keep competing explanations, unresolved variables, and evidence that does not fit.

## Engineering tests

Synthetic tests cover canonical Evidence ID and Observation linkage, Study consistency, source locator validation, extraction verification, independent prospective Study and DOI requirements, contradiction retention, hypothesis-only output boundaries, interaction and bundle hashes, optional robustness/response-surface linkage, input immutability, and output-directory protection. These are software checks only; they do not constitute scientific acceptance on the real literature corpus.
